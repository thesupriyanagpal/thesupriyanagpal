## 🚀 About Me

I'm a BI and analytics consultant with a PhD in Mathematics. I build dashboards and models that help teams understand what's changing in their operations and where to look next.

My doctoral research combined queueing theory and fuzzy set theory to model waiting times in emergency department triage when arrival and service rates are uncertain. I bring that same curiosity about uncertainty to business analytics, from defining a KPI to checking what a dashboard can tell us.

Outside of analytics, I write poetry and enjoy finding ways to bring mathematics into storytelling.
